Fabric Composition

Choosing Between Airflow and Full Waterproofing Shades

Breathable

HDPE, 320–340 gsm

A knitted mesh that blocks around 98% of UV rays while letting air move freely beneath the canopy.

Waterproof

PVC-Coated Polyester, 650–850 gsm

A fully sealed, 100% waterproof membrane built for permanent commercial installations that need complete rain cover.

Premium

PTFE (Teflon-Coated)

A self-cleaning, long-life fabric reserved for landmark permanent structures where finish and longevity outweigh upfront cost.

Layout and Installation Guidance

Triangle Shade Sail Layout

A single or paired triangular sail anchored at three points – the simplest layout for tight corners, small patios, or a single seating area.

Multi-Sail Design Layouts

Combining several sails at varying heights and angles to cover larger areas while adding visual depth rather than one flat panel.

Overlapping Shade Sails

Staggering sails so their edges layer over one another, removing gaps in coverage and increasing shade between panels through the day.

Attaching a Sail to a House Wall

Uses a wall-mounted eye plate rated for the sail's tension load, paired with a turnbuckle so tension can be adjusted after install.

10–15°CAmbient Temperature Drop

Thermal Comfort, Not Just Sun Cover

Breathable HDPE open-mesh fabric avoids the greenhouse effect that solid plastic or sealed covers create. A correctly specified open-mesh shade can drop the ambient temperature underneath it by roughly 10°C to 15°C.
